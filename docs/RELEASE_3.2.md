## PixelPanel Studio 3.2 – animated icons ✨

**New**
- **Animated icons** – switch on “Animate icon” for value, entity and timer pages: the light bulb glows when the light is on (grey when off), the colour light cycles its colours, the washing machine and dryer drums turn, the dishwasher sprays, the fan spins, the hourglass trickles while the timer runs
- Choose what counts as “on”: a separate entity (e.g. the smart plug) and “Running above” for power sensors (e.g. 5 W)
- **Timer: border flashes when done** – optional, colour of your choice
- New icons: **dryer** and **fan**

**Fixed**
- Settings → Panel & hardware → Power supply: the bar now updates immediately while typing the amps

**Download**
- **PC version (Windows/Mac/Linux):** `PixelPanelStudio_3.2.zip` below → unpack → `Start.bat` / `start.sh`
- **Home Assistant app:** updates under *Settings → Apps*
- **Manual:** `PixelPanel_Manual_EN_3.2.pdf` · `PixelPanel_Handbuch_DE_3.2.pdf`

After updating, press **Apply & upload to panel** once so the panel gets the new version.
